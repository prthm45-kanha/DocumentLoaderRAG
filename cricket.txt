On a sunny day or a starry night,
The sound of cricket feels just right.
A bat, a ball, a field of green,
The most beautiful game ever seen.
The bowler runs with all his might,
The batter stands, prepared to fight.
A perfect shot, the ball flies high,
Six runs vanish into the sky!
The crowd erupts, the stadium roars,
Every boundary opens new doors.
A single wicket can change the game,
And every player dreams of fame.
Sometimes we win, sometimes we fall,
But cricket teaches us through it all.
Patience, courage, and the will to fight,
To chase our dreams with all our might.
The last over comes, the tension grows,
The fate of victory, nobody knows.
Eleven hearts beat as one,
Until the final ball is done.
From dusty streets to stadium lights,
From childhood games to thrilling nights,
With every catch and every run,
Cricket brings millions together as one.
It's more than a game, more than a score,
It's the passion that makes us ask for more.
Through every defeat and every victory,
Cricket writes its own beautiful story.
And when the stumps fall, the cheers subside,
The love for cricket will never hide.
For deep inside, one truth remains—
Cricket lives in our hearts and veins! ❤️🏏
